Shipping, returns and care

Shipping and insurance

Every order ships insured and tracked, and needs a signature on delivery. Delivery takes 3 to 5 working days.

Returns and exchanges

Unworn pieces can be exchanged within 7 days with the original invoice and hallmark certificate. Custom and made-to-order pieces are not returnable.

Jewellery care

Wipe with a soft dry cloth. Keep pieces apart in their pouches and remove them before swimming or applying perfume.
